Questions

An open-source revival of a project that went offline.

What is this?
Bee Home was released by SPACE10, IKEA's research lab, on 20 May 2020 — World Bee Day. A parametric bee hotel anyone could configure and have cut locally, free, under CC BY 4.0. SPACE10 closed in 2023 and the site went offline with it. This rebuild was started in July 2026, working from the project's own geometry file and a copy of the original site recovered from the Wayback Machine. What is ours and what is theirs is listed below.
What is original and what did you rebuild?
The storeys are the project's own solids, meshed straight from BEEHOME GEOMETRIES.3dm, and the stacking rules are the recovered ones. Ours: the roof slab, because it was generated at download time by a Grasshopper definition that was never published; the timber choices, which the original did not offer; and this site around the builder.
Will bees actually come?
Often, though nobody can promise it. Cavity-nesting solitary bees find new nest sites on their own. Flowers within about 300 metres matter more than anything you can do to the box.
How long does one last?
Five to thirty years, depending on the timber, where it hangs and whether you clean it. Brush the fronts each autumn and clear any cavity that stayed empty two seasons running.
Do they sting?
Practically never. Solitary bees make no honey and have no colony to defend, so they have nothing to be aggressive about. They sting only if trodden on or squeezed, and are safe around children and pets.
Can I use this?
Yes. CC BY 4.0, same as the original — build it, sell what you build, fork the code, just credit SPACE10 and the designers. The repository is public.

Plant for them

Plant flowers within flying distance.

A red mason bee will forage up to about 600 metres from her nest, but when flowers are close she works within 50 to 300, and averages nearer 100. Every trip is pollen carried home. She packs each cell with a loaf of it, lays one egg on top and seals the cell — that is provisioning, and it is all the food that larva will ever get. Flowers by the box are the difference between a female provisioning ten cells and provisioning thirty.

Tell us roughly where you are. We'll name the cavity-nesting bees recorded near you, what they pollinate locally, and which seed to sow.

Solitary bees are friendly. They don't produce honey and therefore have nothing to protect. They only sting if trod on, and are safe to keep around kids and pets.

Bees see ultraviolet, and many flowers print landing guides in it. These plates are bee-vision simulations, mapping the bee's ultraviolet, blue and green receptors onto blue, green and red so a human screen can show them — a legend, not what she sees. Where no ultraviolet pattern is documented for a species we have left it plain rather than invent one.
