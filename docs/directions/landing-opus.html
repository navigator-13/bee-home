Download the design files for free and share them with a makerspace.

We'll help you find a local space, and they'll help you create your Bee Home. Most makerspaces have computer-controlled tools, including a CNC milling machine, which is what you'll need to fabricate it.

A makerspace is an open workshop with access to a variety of fabrication tools. Think of a gym, but for makers, where you either have a membership or pay per visit. You also get a two-page spec sheet: cut list, elevations and the assembly order.

Assembly needs your hands, a drill and two screws

Place it

Facing the morning sun, within 300 metres of flowers.

Place it anywhere, as long as it's outside. Make sure your Bee Home is protected from strong winds and that the holes remain horizontal at all times.

Assembling it requires just your hands and a few minutes of your time. To mount it on a wall, simply use a drill and two screws.

Questions

An open-source revival of a project that went offline.

What is this?
Bee Home was released by SPACE10, IKEA's research lab, on World Bee Day 2020 — a parametric bee hotel anyone could configure and have cut locally, free, under CC BY 4.0. SPACE10 closed in 2023 and the site went with it. This is a rebuild of the design tool from the original geometry.
Is this the real thing?
The storeys are the project's own solids, meshed straight from BEEHOME GEOMETRIES.3dm. The stacking rules are the recovered ones. Two things are ours: the roof slab, which was generated at download time by a Grasshopper definition we do not have, and the timber options, which the original did not offer.
Will bees actually come?
Often, though nobody can promise it. Cavity-nesting solitary bees find new nest sites on their own. Flowers within about 300 metres matter more than anything you can do to the box.
How long does one last?
Five to thirty years, depending on the timber, where it hangs and whether you clean it. Brush the fronts each autumn and clear any cavity that stayed empty two seasons running.
Do they sting?
Practically never. Solitary bees make no honey and have no colony to defend, so they have nothing to be aggressive about. They sting only if trodden on or squeezed, and are safe around children and pets.
What do the numbers mean?
Bores run 4 to 10 mm, and different species use different diameters, so a mixed stack houses more of them. Each nesting female lays 20 to 30 offspring, and one solitary bee can pollinate as much as 120 honeybees.
Can I use this?
Yes. CC BY 4.0, same as the original — build it, sell what you build, fork the code, just credit SPACE10 and the designers. The repository is public.
